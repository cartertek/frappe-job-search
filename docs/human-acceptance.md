# Human acceptance tests: Job Search

Use **synthetic test data** and the signed-in accounts with their actual assigned roles. Open `/desk/job-search` and the Job, Search and Organization lists. Do not impersonate any user or create a human approval on their behalf.

1. **One status:** Open a Job and verify it has one **Status** field using only Discovered, Researching, Ready to Review, Qualified, Applied, Interviewing, Accepted or Rejected; no `JS:` stage or removed legacy status.
2. **Job form:** Verify Overview (with editable Job Title) and Metadata are side by side, followed by Job Search Fields, Job Details, Research Heuristics and collapsed Research Notes. The Active Job Search link and research-heuristic table are editable; the metadata fields retain their values. The Research Heuristics grid has distinct, aligned **Heuristic** and **Value** columns, including row numbers, and displays long Values across multiple lines without clipping.
3. **Read-only form open:** Open and refresh a saved Job with an Active Job Search whose heuristics have changed. Confirm the form is **not marked unsaved/edited** merely by opening or refreshing, and its research assessments are not silently recreated. Use the explicit Sync action or change the Active Job Search to update rows.
5. **Heuristic synchronization:** Select a Search with reference job titles and multiple named custom heuristics; check that the built-in Job Title heuristic lists the reference titles and that each custom criterion appears with its description and an editable Value. Reorder search criteria; previously entered values must remain associated with the correct criterion. Changing searches must warn before discarding values for removed criteria.
5. **Review-readiness errors:** From Researching, attempt **Ready to Review** with no Active Job Search, a disabled search, a mismatched title/location/type/arrangement/seniority/date/compensation filter, and a missing or empty heuristic Value. Verify the action stays blocked and reports specific failures. Test duplicate and stale heuristic rows; none may pass.
6. **Review-readiness success:** Supply a matching active search, all required values, and any explicit Include required by that search; verify Researching → Ready to Review succeeds. A search Include that admits a filter-mismatched job to search results must **not** bypass this stage gate. Verify the API cannot bypass it either.
7. **Human approval:** Confirm **Mark Qualified** requires a signed-in human approver's **Pursue** decision and rationale, cited research and a completed research procedure; simply populating heuristic values does not qualify a job. **Needs Research** returns it to Researching.
8. **Later stages:** **Mark Applied** requires authorized confirmed application submission and materials; **Begin Interviewing** requires an interview record; **Accept Offer** requires offer details and an explicit human Accept Offer decision with rationale.
9. **Rejection scopes:** Verify **Search Only** excludes the Job from the selected search and marks its Status **Rejected** without globally rejecting it, **Job Globally** flags and rejects the job, and **Entire Organization** rejects the organization without automatically changing every individual Job's status. Confirm agent rejection rationale is recorded under the actual operator in the Job timeline, status transitions are retained, and no human approval decision is fabricated.
10. **Permissions:** An operator without approver rights **can Reject during research** and can send a completed qualifying opportunity to Ready to Review, but cannot issue a human Pursue/Accept Offer decision. Verify the actual approver can use the allowed actions; no account impersonation.
11. **Related data:** Confirm linked postings, revisions, research, applications, interview records, tasks, comments and attachments are still accessible after migration.

Browser-based UI checks are separate from schema and API tests. See [Job status workflow](workflows.md) for exact requirements and edge-case transitions.

11. **Claim evidence:** Create two sourced claims for the same heuristic; ensure they are accepted. Verify a missing quote, missing source URL or missing required heuristic blocks Ready to Review, and a legacy value without evidence does not satisfy the gate. Check that historical assessment text is appended to Research Findings without replacing existing text during migration.

12. **Legacy assessment migration:** After upgrade, verify historical heuristic answers appear in Research Findings without replacing existing notes; no empty legacy child rows or legacy value/description columns remain. New claims contain only Heuristic, Exact Quote, and Source URL.
