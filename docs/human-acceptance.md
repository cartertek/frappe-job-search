# Human acceptance tests — Opportunity / Job split (issue #15)

Run against a **copy of production data** first. Work only as the dedicated assistant/operator account; human approvals must be issued by the actual human approver, never impersonated. Preserve full posted evidence and all historical data.

1. **Independent collections:** Workspace and sidebar show **Opportunities**, **Jobs**, **Organizations**, **Searches**, and **Research Strategies**. Opening a Job shows title, employer, full description, Job Postings, compensation/location/date, heuristic claims, and *Availability* (Unknown, Active, Closed, Rejected); it does **not** have the pursuit-stage Status. Opening an Opportunity shows its Name, Organization, Jobs table, Active Job Search, Current Eligibility, and pursuit Status.
2. **Historical migration:** Every old Job retains its document ID under DocType **Job**; each gets a same-ID **Opportunity** initially, with a linked child row. Compare all existing Job descriptions, claims, employers, postings, research, sources, tasks, transitions, decisions, approvals, timestamps, and file/activity references against backup. Different historical pursuits are **not** auto-merged.
3. **Ordinary grouping:** Create two Jobs at a new Organization; both should attach to its single default Opportunity. Create a separate division Opportunity and add a Job; permit multiple Opportunity links without duplicate Job rows. When several Opportunities exist, creating a Job must require an explicit selection.
4. **Search matching:** A Search returns matching Opportunities with the matching Job IDs; multiple Searches may match one Opportunity. Include/Exclude rows target Opportunities. Removing from one Search does not affect other Searches. Organization/Opportunity global rejection overrides Include; independently rejected/Closed Jobs do not qualify.
5. **Qualification gate:** Confirm a Search is enabled; set Opportunity Active Job Search. A Job must be Active, meet all configured deterministic/recency filters, have the complete original description and a captured Posting, and supply required dated, sourced heuristic claims. Missing required facts must block Ready to Review. An Include override must **not** bypass the gate. A second qualifying Job may satisfy the same Opportunity when the first does not.
6. **Changing Job evidence:** If the only qualifying Job becomes Closed or Rejected, Ready to Review returns to Researching. If an Opportunity is already Applied/Interviewing/Accepted, do not erase that history; mark Current Eligibility Needs Research. Reopening a Job sets Unknown, not automatically Active.
7. **Pursuit transitions:** Begin Research, Ready to Review, human Qualified, Applied, Interviewing, Accepted follow stage-specific evidence and approvals. Cited research and completed procedures are required for Qualified unless explicitly overridden with authorized evidence. Application, interview, and offer records belong to Opportunity, not Job.
8. **Rejections:** Search Only adds only a Search exclusion, preserving the shared Opportunity stage; clear it by a reasoned operator action. Opportunity Globally rejects that pursuit. Entire Organization rejects the Organization and blocks its Opportunities. Rejecting an individual Job invalidates only that Job. Confirm rationale and signed-in operator attribution, no fabricated human approval, and independent reopening.
9. **Research Strategies:** Assigned instructions load and order by effective priority with per-Search overrides. Do not confuse a strategy assignment with a heuristic claim.
10. **API, forms, and cache:** Verify `register_job`, `register_opportunity`, `associate_job`, `get_search_results`, `set_search_membership`, `capture_posting`, actions and queues. Search results and client links use the new Opportunity DocType. Desk FormMeta, sidebar, Workspace, desktop layout and Wiki remain accessible after migration. Check both logged-out availability and operator/approver permissions.
11. **Idempotency:** Run migrate twice; no duplicate Opportunities or associations, no lost records. Run strict desktop integrity verification and verify site health. Do **not** close the issue before full documentation updates and real-site regression checks succeed.
